| Condition | Adder | Unit | Notes |

## Quoting Rules

- **Scope:** Whole house sqft unless quoting a single room
- **Coats:** 2-coat is base. 3-coat adds $1.00/sqft
- **Minimum job:** None — "you pick it, we stick it"
- **Furnished surcharge:** Applied to total home sqft, not just painted area
- **Market adjustment:** Additive — higher market = higher base rate
